# Project Ledger

A clean, minimal ledger for tracking posts (videos and designs), payments and PDF receipts. Amounts are in Sri Lankan rupees (LKR).

- Add posts with a name, cost and date
- Record payments with an amount, date and method
- Statuses (Paid, Part paid, Not paid) are worked out automatically, oldest posts first
- Transaction history with a running balance
- Download payment receipts and statements as clean PDFs with your logo (print them yourself from the PDF)
- One-time projects: separate jobs with their own client, price, payments and PDF receipts
- Light and dark theme
- Data is stored in your browser. Use Details → Download backup to keep a copy.
